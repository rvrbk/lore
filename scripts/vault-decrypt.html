Open a Lore vault export

Decrypts a lore-vault-….csv.sealed file with your private key. Everything happens inside this page on your own computer: nothing is uploaded, and the page cannot reach the internet.

The result is a plain, unencrypted CSV with all your passwords. Import it into your password manager, then delete it and the .sealed file. Do not open it in a spreadsheet. Needs a current browser (Chrome 133+, Edge 133+, Firefox 130+, Safari 17+).
